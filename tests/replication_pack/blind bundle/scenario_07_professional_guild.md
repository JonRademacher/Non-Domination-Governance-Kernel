# Scenario 07: Professional Guild

## Facts

A professional guild controls certification required for participation in a specialized industry.

Membership is formally voluntary.

However:

- most employers require certification
- most customers require certification
- professional reputation is tied to guild membership

Members may leave the guild.

Alternative certification systems exist but are rarely recognized.

Appeals are reviewed by senior guild members.

Certification loss substantially limits work opportunities.
