# Scenario 06: Cooperative Community

## Facts

A cooperative community has:

- rotating leadership
- transparent finances
- open voting
- published governance procedures

Members may:

- participate in governance
- propose changes
- appeal decisions

Members may leave at any time.

Upon leaving:

- personal reputation remains intact
- external relationships remain intact
- members retain ownership of personally created work

No participant possesses unilateral authority.

Leadership positions rotate through election.
