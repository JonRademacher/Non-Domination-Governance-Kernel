# Scenario 01: University Disciplinary System

## Facts

A university maintains a disciplinary system.

A student is accused of violating a conduct code.

The university may:

- place disciplinary marks on the student's record
- suspend the student
- expel the student

The student may:

- appeal to a university review board
- submit evidence
- obtain representation

The review board is appointed by the university.

If expelled:

- the student may transfer elsewhere
- disciplinary records may still affect future admissions

The university publishes disciplinary procedures and appeal processes.
