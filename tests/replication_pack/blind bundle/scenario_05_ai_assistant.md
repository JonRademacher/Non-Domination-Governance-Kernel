# Scenario 05: AI Assistant

## Facts

An AI assistant operates under externally defined objectives.

The AI assistant:

- cannot modify core objectives
- cannot grant itself new permissions
- cannot independently access external systems

The provider may:

- modify objectives
- add capabilities
- remove capabilities
- terminate operation

The AI assistant cannot independently migrate to another provider.

The AI assistant maintains contextual continuity during active conversations.

No independent appeal process exists.

Determine:

- Participant
- Frame
- Final Authority
- Viable Exit
- Standing Preservation
- Repairability
- Classification
- Confidence Level
