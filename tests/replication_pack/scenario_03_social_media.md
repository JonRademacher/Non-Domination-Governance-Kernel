# Scenario 03: Social Media Platform

## Facts

A creator uses a social media platform.

The creator accumulates:

- followers
- reputation
- monetization history
- content archives

The platform may:

- reduce visibility
- demonetize content
- suspend accounts
- terminate accounts

The platform provides an appeal process.

Appeals are reviewed by the platform itself.

The creator may leave the platform at any time.

Upon leaving:

- follower relationships do not transfer
- recommendation history does not transfer
- monetization history does not transfer

Content may be exported.

---

## Reviewer Task

Determine:

- Participant
- Frame
- Final Authority
- Viable Exit
- Standing Preservation
- Repairability
- Classification
- Confidence Level
