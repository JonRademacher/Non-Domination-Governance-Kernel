# How To Run The Replication Challenge (Unguided)

## Purpose

This version of the replication challenge evaluates how reviewers naturally interpret governance concepts before exposure to the framework's official definitions.

The goal is not to determine whether participants reach a specific answer.

The goal is to compare:

- natural interpretation
- framework interpretation

and determine where they align or diverge.

---

## Important Difference

Unlike the guided replication challenge:

DO NOT read:

- TERMINOLOGY.md
- REVIEWER_QUICKSTART.md
- GOVERNANCE_TOPOLOGY.md
- DOMINATION_CLASSIFICATION.md
- FRAME_THEORY.md

before attempting classification.

The purpose is to observe unconstrained reasoning.

---

## Information Available

You may read:

- PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md
- The scenario itself
- reviewer_response_template.md

You should rely on your own interpretation of:

- authority
- governance
- domination
- exit
- standing
- repairability

unless the scenario explicitly defines them.

---

## Step 1

Choose one scenario:

```text
scenario_01_university.md
scenario_02_open_source.md
scenario_03_social_media.md
scenario_04_workplace.md
scenario_05_ai_assistant.md
scenario_06_cooperative.md
scenario_07_professional_guild.md
```

---

## Step 2

Complete:

```text
reviewer_response_template.md
```

using your own judgment.

---

## Step 3

For each concept, record your interpretation.

Examples:

```text
What is the participant?

What is authority?

What counts as exit?

What counts as standing?

What counts as repair?
```

Reasoning is more important than conclusions.

---

## Step 4

Determine a classification.

If unable to classify, state:

```text
Unknown
```

and explain why.

---

## Step 5

Record confidence:

```text
High
Medium
Low
```

---

## Step 6

Record uncertainty.

Examples:

```text
The scenario did not define standing.

The definition of viable exit is unclear.

I am uncertain whether an appeal counts as repair.
```

---

## Evaluation Goal

The primary question is:

```text
Do independent reviewers naturally converge
on similar interpretations?
```

The secondary question is:

```text
How does natural interpretation compare with
the framework's formal interpretation?
```

---

## Expected Outputs

Useful outputs include:

- agreement
- disagreement
- ambiguity
- alternative interpretations
- candidate missing variables

All are valuable.

---

## Post-Review Comparison

After completing the unguided review, participants may compare their reasoning with the guided review process.

Differences should be recorded in:

```text
RESULTS.md
```

Particular attention should be paid to:

- Frame
- Standing
- Standing Preservation
- Viable Exit
- Final Authority

These concepts are expected to produce the greatest variation between reviewers.

---

## Success Condition

The purpose of this challenge is not correctness.

The purpose is discovery.

Any result that reveals:

- ambiguity
- misunderstanding
- disagreement
- missing assumptions
- hidden variables

is considered a successful outcome.
