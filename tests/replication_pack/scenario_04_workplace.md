# Scenario 04: Workplace Governance

## Facts

An employee works for a large organization.

Management controls:

- role assignment
- performance review
- promotion eligibility
- termination decisions

The employee may:

- resign
- seek employment elsewhere

Departure may result in:

- income disruption
- benefit loss
- loss of internal professional standing

The organization provides:

- HR review
- appeal channels
- anti-retaliation policies

Professional skills remain portable outside the organization.

---

## Reviewer Task

Determine:

- Participant
- Frame
- Final Authority
- Viable Exit
- Standing Preservation
- Repairability
- Classification
- Confidence Level
