# How To Run The Replication Challenge

## Purpose

This replication pack exists to test whether independent reviewers can apply the Non-Domination Governance Kernel consistently.

The goal is not to prove the framework correct.

The goal is to determine:

- whether reviewers can understand the framework
- whether reviewers classify cases similarly
- where disagreements occur
- whether missing variables exist
- whether additional clarification is required

---

## What You Need To Read

Before attempting the replication challenge, read:

1. PROJECT_OVERVIEW.md
2. REVIEWER_QUICKSTART.md
3. TERMINOLOGY.md

Optional:

4. THREATS_TO_VALIDITY.md

Do not read repository issues before attempting classification.

Do not consult repository authors.

The purpose is to simulate an independent review.

---

## Step 1

Select one scenario from:

```text
scenario_01_university.md
scenario_02_open_source.md
scenario_03_social_media.md
scenario_04_workplace.md
scenario_05_ai_assistant.md
scenario_06_cooperative.md
scenario_07_professional_guild.md
```

---

## Step 2

Complete:

```text
reviewer_response_template.md
```

for the selected scenario.

---

## Step 3

Attempt independent classification.

Determine:

- Participant
- Frame
- Final Authority
- Viable Exit
- Standing Preservation
- Repairability
- Classification
- Confidence

---

## Step 4

If you cannot determine a classification, explicitly state:

```text
Unknown
```

and explain why.

An unknown classification is a valid result.

---

## Step 5

Record your response.

Appropriate outputs include:

```text
Open Governance

Bounded Governance

Repairable Domination

Closed Domination

Recursive Domination

Unknown
```

---

## Step 6

Identify disagreement points.

Examples:

```text
Frame ambiguity

Standing ambiguity

Exit ambiguity

Authority ambiguity

Missing structural variable
```

---

## Evaluation Criteria

The replication challenge is successful if:

- reviewers can explain their reasoning
- disagreement points become visible
- missing variables become identifiable

Perfect agreement is not required.

---

## What To Do With Results

Record results in:

```text
RESULTS.md
```

Compare:

- classifications
- confidence levels
- disagreement sources

The most important output is not agreement.

The most important output is understanding why disagreement occurred.
