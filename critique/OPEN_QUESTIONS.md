# Open Questions

## 0. Purpose

This file records unresolved questions that should guide future development.

## 1. Minimal Frame Threshold

What is the weakest sufficient condition for `HasFrame(P)`?

Open issue:

```text
How much continuity, interpretation, transformation capacity, and boundary recognition are required?
```

## 2. Degrees of Exit Viability

Can exit be graded rather than binary?

Open issue:

```text
ViableExitFrom(P,G) ∈ [0,1]
```

may better represent partial portability, partial standing preservation, and partial repair.

## 3. Domination Debt

Can domination severity be measured without reducing the theory to arbitrary scoring?

Open issue:

```text
Debt(P,G)
```

should preserve structural meaning.

## 4. Repair Effectiveness

What counts as a successful repair?

Open issue:

```text
Repair must reduce domination debt, but how should that reduction be represented?
```

## 5. Collective Frames

Can groups, institutions, communities, or organizations have frames under the kernel?

Open issue:

```text
How are collective frames distinguished from aggregates of individual frames?
```

## 6. Temporal Domination

Can a relation alternate between domination and non-domination over time?

Open issue:

```text
D_t(P,G) may vary across time.
```

## 7. Internalized Authority

What happens when external authority becomes internalized into the participant frame?

Open issue:

```text
Authority localization may become historically layered.
```

## 8. Competing Authorities

How should the kernel classify multiple authorities acting on the same frame?

Open issue:

```text

```

## 9. Necessary Domination

Are there cases where domination topology is unavoidable or justified by emergency conditions?

Open issue:

```text

```

## 10. Legal and Moral Translation

How should structural domination classification map, if at all, into legal duty, moral duty, or rights language?

Open issue:
