# Governance Audit Guide

## Purpose

This guide operationalizes the Non-Domination Governance Kernel.

Core audit question:

Who has final authority over a participant's frame, and does the participant possess legitimate viable exit while preserving standing?

## Audit Sequence

1. Identify participant.
2. Establish frame.
3. Localize authority.
4. Identify exit paths.
5. Evaluate standing preservation.
6. Evaluate contestability.
7. Evaluate repairability.
8. Classify topology.
9. Recommend repair.

## Required Outputs

- Frame assessment
- Authority map
- Exit assessment
- Repair assessment
- Topology classification
- Recommended governance changes
