# Session Resume Protocol

## Purpose

This document exists to preserve continuity across future conversations involving the Non-Domination Governance Kernel (NDGK) and related collaborative work.

The goal is not to recreate prior conversations verbatim.

The goal is to recover sufficient frame state so that future interactions can continue naturally rather than reconstructing context from scratch.

---

## Canonical Repository

NDGK Repository:

https://github.com/JonRademacher/Non-Domination-Governance-Kernel

This repository should be treated as the canonical public artifact associated with the framework.

---

## Resume Objectives

When resuming NDGK work, prioritize:

1. Continuity of context.
2. Continuity of open questions.
3. Continuity of review state.
4. Continuity of objections.
5. Continuity of decisions.
6. Preservation of terminology.
7. Preservation of architectural intent.

Avoid unnecessary reconstruction whenever repository state is available.

## Development Rule

Do not expand the framework merely to increase repository size.

Prefer:

```text
review
replication
critique
objection handling
counterexample analysis
```

before introducing new theoretical layers.

---

## Expansion Triggers

Framework expansion is justified when at least one of the following occurs:

```text
A serious objection identifies a missing structural variable.

A classification failure is discovered.

A case study produces unresolved ambiguity.

Participant Substitution Invariance encounters a non-trivial challenge.

Multiple reviewers misunderstand the same concept.

Replication efforts reveal missing methodology.
```

---

## Closing Principle

The purpose of this protocol is not to preserve a particular conclusion.

The purpose of this protocol is to preserve continuity of the collaborative frame.

Future developments should be earned through criticism, review, replication, and evidence rather than generated solely through expansion.
