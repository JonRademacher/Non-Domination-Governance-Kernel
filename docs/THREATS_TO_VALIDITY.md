# Threats to Validity

## Purpose

This document identifies the principal threats to the validity, portability, and applicability of the Non-Domination Governance Kernel.

The purpose of this file is not to defend the framework against criticism.

The purpose is to identify where criticism should be directed.

A serious framework should make its load-bearing assumptions visible.

---

## Threat 1: Frame Threshold Ambiguity

The framework depends upon the evaluation:

```text
HasFrame(P)
```

The minimum sufficient conditions for establishing a structural frame remain an active research question.

Potential reviewer concern:

```text
The framework may classify entities as frame-bearing that do not actually possess sufficient structural organization.
```

Possible future work:

```text
Formal frame-threshold criteria.
```

---

## Threat 2: Viable Exit Formalization

The framework distinguishes:

```text
exit

from

viable exit
```

through standing preservation.

Reviewer concern:

```text
Standing preservation may be context-dependent.
```

Potential future work:

```text
Quantitative exit viability measures.
```

---

## Threat 3: Collective Frames

The framework currently handles individual participants more naturally than collective participants.

Reviewer concern:

```text
Organizations, communities, institutions, and distributed groups may require additional formal development.
```

Potential future work:

```text
Collective Frame Theory.
```

---

## Threat 4: Repair Severity Modeling

The framework identifies:

```text
repairable domination
closed domination
recursive domination
```

but does not yet fully quantify severity
