# Issue Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document describes how issues progress through the Non-Domination Governance Kernel review process.

The goal is to transform criticism into structured evidence rather than allowing issues to accumulate without resolution.

---

## States

### Open

A question, objection, counterexample, or ambiguity has been identified.

Examples:

- Missing variable
- Classification disagreement
- Replication inconsistency
- Ambiguous definition

---

### Reviewing

The issue is being evaluated.

Questions:

- Is the issue reproducible?
- Is the issue well specified?
- Is additional information required?

---

### Replication Required

The issue is sufficiently important that it should be tested.

Actions:

- Create a replication scenario if necessary.
- Direct reviewers to the replication pack.
- Compare independent responses.

---

### Resolved

The issue has been addressed through:

- clarification
- documentation
- definition refinement
- correction

No revision to the framework is required.

---

### Theory Revision Required

The issue survived review and replication.

Framework modification may be necessary.

This state may trigger future release activity.

---

## Workflow

Open
↓
Reviewing
↓
Replication Required
↓
Resolved

or

Open
↓
Reviewing
↓
Replication Required
↓
Theory Revision Required
