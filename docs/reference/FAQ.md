# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is this an AI rights framework?
No. The kernel classifies governance structures.

## Does domination imply consciousness?
No.

## Does the framework prove personhood?
No.

## Why use frames?
Frames provide a portable structural object across domains.

## Why focus on viable exit?
Because governance and domination differ most clearly when exit is examined.

## Why not use autonomy alone?
Autonomy and domination are different concepts.

## What is Participant Substitution Invariance?
It is the theorem stating that domination classification remains unchanged when domination-relevant structural variables are preserved.
