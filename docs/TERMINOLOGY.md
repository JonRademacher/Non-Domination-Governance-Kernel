# Terminology

Frame
: Structured interpretive-governance context.

Final Authority
: Authority determining admissibility of relevant frame transformations.

Viable Exit
: Standing-preserving path out of a governance relation.

Governance
: Authority relation organizing admissibility and coordination.

Domination
: Final frame authority combined with absence of viable exit.

Repair
: Structural modification reducing domination conditions.

Open Governance
: No final authority over the evaluated frame.

Bounded Governance
: Final authority exists but viable exit remains available.

Repairable Domination
: Domination exists but meaningful repair remains available.

Closed Domination
: Domination exists without repair.

Recursive Domination
: Apparently available repair routes return to the same final authority.
