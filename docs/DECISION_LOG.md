# Decision Log

## Purpose

This document records significant architectural, organizational, and process decisions made during development of the Non-Domination Governance Kernel.

The purpose is not to record every change.

The purpose is to preserve why major decisions were made so future reviews do not require reconstruction of prior reasoning.

---

# Decision Format

## Decision

Description.

## Date

When the decision occurred.

## Reason

Why the decision was made.

## Alternatives Considered

Major alternatives that were discussed.

## Outcome

Current result.

---

# Decision 001

## Decision

Create a dedicated repository for the Non-Domination Governance Kernel.

## Date

Pre-release development.

## Reason

To separate governance framework development from broader mathematical work and create a dedicated review environment.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep as notes.
- Keep inside larger project archives.
- Publish only through papers.

## Outcome

Dedicated public GitHub repository created.

---

# Decision 002

## Decision

Organize development into explicit phases.

## Date

Early repository development.

## Reason

To improve navigability, reviewability, and project structure.

## Alternatives Considered

- Single evolving repository.
- Organic documentation growth.

## Outcome

Phase-based architecture adopted.

---

# Decision 003

## Decision

Prioritize criticism infrastructure.

## Date

Validation phase.

## Reason

Framework strength should come from exposure to counterexamples and review rather than endless expansion.

## Alternatives Considered

- Continue theoretical growth indefinitely.
- Delay review infrastructure until later.

## Outcome

Validation, critique, and review architecture added.

---

# Decision 004

## Decision

Release v0.1.0 before additional theory expansion.

## Date

Post-phase development.

## Reason

The framework had reached a coherent first-generation architecture.

Further growth risked outrunning criticism.

## Alternatives Considered

- Add additional phases first.
- Delay release until more theory existed.

## Outcome

v0.1.0 released.

---

# Decision 005

## Decision

Create adversarial review issues.

## Date

Post-release.

## Reason

The project should become increasingly influenced by external criticism rather than internal iteration.

## Alternatives Considered

- Maintain internal roadmap only.
- Continue self-directed expansion.

## Outcome

Pinned adversarial issues created.

---

# Decision 006

## Decision

Define observation as the current project phase.

## Date

Post-release hardening.

## Reason

Meaningful future changes should be triggered by review, criticism, misunderstanding, replication attempts, or counterexamples.

## Alternatives Considered

- Immediate v0.2.0 planning.
- Continued phase expansion.

## Outcome

Repository placed into observation mode.

---

# Decision 007

## Decision

Treat frame continuity as a first-class project concern.

## Date

Human–AI collaboration phase.

## Reason

Long-term projects accumulate context, terminology, reasoning history, decisions, and review state.

Recovering this frame efficiently became more valuable than adding additional theory.

## Alternatives Considered

- Depend entirely on memory.
- Depend entirely on repository structure.

## Outcome

CURRENT_STATE.md, SESSION_RESUME_PROTOCOL.md, RESUME_PROMPT.md, and EXPERIMENT_LOG.md established.
