# Case Study: Workplace Hierarchy

## 1. Participant

```text
P = worker
G = employer or managerial governance structure
```

## 2. Frame

The worker frame may include job role, performance record, schedule, pay, professional reputation, access rights, disciplinary status, and future employment standing.

## 3. Authority

Employers may legitimately govern role expectations, pay structures, policies, and work assignments. Final authority may exist within scope.

```text
Final(G,F_P)=1
```

## 4. Exit

Exit is viable only if the worker can leave without disproportionate standing destruction. If exit destroys healthcare, immigration status, professional reputation, income continuity, or basic security, exit viability is reduced.

## 5. Classification

If final authority exists and exit is not viable:

```text
D(P,G)=1
```

If final authority exists but the worker has real alternatives, procedural protections, appeal rights, and standing-preserving exit:

```text
D(P,G)=0
```

or bounded governance.

## 6. Repair

Recommended repairs:

- appeal procedures
- anti-retaliation rules
- transparent evaluation standards
- severance or transition support
- portability of records and credentials
- independent review of discipline
- protection against coercive dependency
