# Case Study: Administrative Agency

## 1. Participant

```text
P = regulated subject, applicant, beneficiary, or respondent
G = administrative agency or tribunal
```

## 2. Frame

The participant frame may include eligibility status, compliance record, benefits, license status, legal standing, enforcement posture, or administrative classification.

## 3. Authority

An agency may have final or near-final authority over classifications and outcomes unless checked by appeal, judicial review, or procedural rights.

## 4. Exit

Exit is viable only if the participant can appeal, seek independent review, preserve standing, correct records, and avoid irreversible deprivation without remedy.

## 5. Classification

If the agency controls the participant's formal status and no meaningful appeal or standing-preserving review exists:

```text
D(P,G)=1
```

If review, due process, and remedy are available:

```text
bounded governance
```

## 6. Repair

Recommended repairs:

- notice
- reasons
- hearing opportunity
- appeal rights
- independent review
- record correction
- proportionality checks
- remedy after erroneous classification
