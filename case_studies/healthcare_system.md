# Case Study: Healthcare Governance

## 1. Participant

```text
P = patient
G = care institution, insurer, state program, or clinical governance structure
```

## 2. Frame

The patient frame may include diagnosis, treatment options, medical record, consent status, care priority, eligibility, financial exposure, and institutional classification.

## 3. Authority

Clinical systems may have significant authority over diagnosis, treatment access, record interpretation, and care pathways.

```text
Final(G,F_P)=1
```

may hold within specific scopes.

## 4. Exit

Exit is viable only if the patient can seek second opinion, transfer care, correct records, appeal denial, or refuse treatment without disproportionate loss of care standing.

## 5. Classification

Domination risk exists when:

```text
Final(G,F_P)=1
ViableExitFrom(P,G)=0
```

especially where treatment, record, or eligibility authority is final and unrepairable.

## 6. Repair

Recommended repairs:

- informed consent
- second opinion access
- record correction rights
- appeal of denial
- non-retaliatory transfer rights
- patient advocate process
- transparent criteria for care decisions
