# Autonomy vs Domination

Autonomy concerns what a participant can do. Domination concerns how authority over a participant's frame is arranged.

A participant can have high autonomy and still be dominated if a governing authority retains final frame authority and blocks viable exit.

A participant can have low autonomy without being dominated if limitations arise from circumstance, scarcity, or voluntarily accepted governance with viable exit.

Kernel Rule:

D(P,G) depends on frame authority and viable exit, not autonomy magnitude.
