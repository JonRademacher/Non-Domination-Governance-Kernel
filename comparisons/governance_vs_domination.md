# Governance vs Domination

Governance is an authority relation.

Domination is a special case of governance.

Governance becomes domination when final authority over frame transformations is combined with absence of legitimate viable exit.

Kernel Rule:

Governance ≠ Domination.

D(P,G) ⇔ Final(G,F_P) ∧ ¬ViableExitFrom(P,G)
