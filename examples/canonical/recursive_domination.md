# Canonical Example: Recursive Domination

## Purpose

This example represents domination that reproduces itself through governance structures.

---

## Scenario

A professional accreditation organization controls:

- certification
- appeals
- review boards
- eligibility requirements

Loss of certification prevents meaningful participation in the profession.

Appeals are reviewed exclusively by certified members selected by existing leadership.

Governance rules cannot be challenged outside the existing structure.

Future decision makers are chosen by the same authority structure being reviewed.

---

## Why This Example Matters

Recursive domination occurs when:

```text
Authority
controls the process that determines
whether authority may be challenged.
```

The governance relation continually reproduces itself through its own review mechanisms.

This represents one of the strongest domination classifications within the framework.
