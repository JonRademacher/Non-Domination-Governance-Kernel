# Canonical Example: Closed Domination

## Purpose

This example represents domination without meaningful repair or viable exit.

---

## Scenario

A participant operates within a governance structure where:

- final decisions are made by a central authority
- decisions are not contestable
- no appeal process exists
- no independent review exists

The participant may technically leave.

However departure results in:

- loss of reputation
- loss of professional standing
- inability to continue equivalent activity

Alternative systems do not realistically exist.

## Why This Example Matters

The framework treats formal exit and viable exit as different concepts.

The existence of a nominal exit path does not imply non-domination.

Standing preservation remains critical.
