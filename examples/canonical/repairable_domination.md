# Canonical Example: Repairable Domination

## Purpose

This example represents a domination relationship that contains viable repair mechanisms.

The purpose is to distinguish domination from irreversible domination.

---

## Scenario

A creator relies on a hosting platform.

The platform possesses final authority over:

- account status
- monetization
- visibility

The creator cannot realistically migrate followers to another platform.

The platform may:

- suspend accounts
- demonetize content
- reduce visibility

However:

- appeal procedures exist
- account restoration exists
- independent review mechanisms exist
- policy decisions may be challenged
