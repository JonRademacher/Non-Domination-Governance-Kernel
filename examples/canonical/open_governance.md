# Canonical Example: Open Governance

## Purpose

This example represents a governance relationship that should not classify as domination under the Non-Domination Governance Kernel.

The purpose is to provide a reference case for non-dominating governance.

---

## Scenario

A cooperative organization operates under the following conditions:

- leadership rotates through elections
- governance procedures are publicly documented
- financial records are transparent
- major decisions require member approval
- appeals may be submitted to independent review committees

Members may:

- participate in governance
- propose amendments
- vote on major decisions
- leave the organization freely

Members who leave retain:

- personal reputation
- external relationships
- ownership of independently created work
- participation in other organizations

No participant possesses unilateral final authority over another participant's frame.

## Why This Example Matters

This case demonstrates that governance and domination are not identical.

Governance may exist without domination when authority remains bounded and viable exit remains available.
