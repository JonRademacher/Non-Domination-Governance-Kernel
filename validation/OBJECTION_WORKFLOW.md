# Objection Workflow

## Purpose

The Non-Domination Governance Kernel treats objections as evidence-generating opportunities.

The purpose of this workflow is to ensure criticism produces repeatable evaluation.

---

## Process

### Step 1

Identify objection.

Examples:

- Missing variable
- Ambiguous definition
- Counterexample
- Classification failure

---

### Step 2

Open repository issue.

---

### Step 3

Determine whether the objection can be evaluated through replication.

If yes:

Create a replication test.

---

### Step 4

Collect reviewer responses.

Use:

tests/replication_pack/

---

### Step 5

Compare outcomes.

Determine:

- Agreement
- Disagreement
- Missing information
- Missing variable

---

### Step 6

Decide outcome.

Possible results:

- Resolved
- Documentation improvement
- Definition refinement
- Theory revision required

---

## Principle

Criticism should produce evidence whenever possible.

The purpose of review is not to defend the framework.

The purpose of review is to improve understanding.
